About PVPSL

The pinnacle of professional soccer in the Pungwe Valley

Our Mission

The Pungwe Valley Professional Soccer League (PVPSL) is dedicated to organizing, promoting, and elevating the standard of professional soccer in the region. We strive to provide a competitive platform for local talent to shine, fostering community spirit, and delivering top-tier sports entertainment to our passionate fans.

Our History

Founded with the vision of uniting the Pungwe Valley through the beautiful game, PVPSL has grown from a small collection of local clubs into a highly professional and fiercely competitive league. Over the years, we have continuously modernized our operations, ensuring fair play, high-quality refereeing, and state-of-the-art facility management.

Core Values

Integrity

Upholding the highest standards of fair play and honesty.

Community

Building strong bonds between clubs, players, and fans.

Excellence

Striving for the highest quality in every match played.
